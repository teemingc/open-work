The OpenCollective invoice for https://opencollective.com/svelte requires an expense title and description:

## Expense title

The expense title should be short, specific, and descriptive. Clearly state what the expense is.

### Examples of previously accepted titles:

> Maintenance and Development (09/2024)

> Maintenance and development in the Svelte org September 2025

> Contributions for Jan/Feb/March 2024

> May contributions to @sveltejs/kit and @sveltejs/svelte

## Expense description

1. Detailed description of the work provided (Clear description of what you did)
2. How it supported the project: Include additional detail about how it relates to the project
3. When you did it (Timeframe that you completed the work)
4. links that support the work completed

To speed up payment processing and improve transparency, consider including relevant links that support the expense (especially important when you're a sole maintainer/admin):

- Changelogs
- Repositories
- Blog posts
- GitHub commits

### Examples of Acceptable Descriptions:

"Fixed critical security bugs in February 2024"
"Maintenance on project infrastructure, March 1-7, 2025"

### Examples of previously accepted titles:

> Software Service (2025/11) / Tee Ming

> Maintenance and development in the Svelte org September 2025

> 26 hours of work from 1st March to 14th March

> 30h on devtools - security maintenance and enhancements to CI/CD pipelines for automatic signing and publishing

> Contributions in January 2024 - 4h

> @sveltejs/kit PRs: #9996, #9994, #9950, #9889, #9947, 10074, #9979, #9949, #9946, #9945, #9944, #9911, #9909, #9908, #9902, #9888, #9863 (+issue triage / management)

## Don't:
Use vague or generic terms without specificity (e.g.: "work", "services", "payment")
Use terms like "grant", "donation", "reward" — those words imply that work wasn't necessary done.

### Examples of Unacceptable Descriptions:
"Work done on the project" 
 "Payment for involvement"
 "Donation for service"
